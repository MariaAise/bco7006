# Assessment 4 — Business Cases

*Business Analytics Solution Modelling: Research Report (BCO7006)*

Each business case is built around one Kaggle competition and a notebook of code that addresses it. Your group will be allocated **one case** and given **two links**:

- **Competition** — the business problem and the data (your scenario).
- **Code** — the notebook your group will analyse and explain in the report.

You do not write code for this assessment. You analyse the allocated notebook and report on it for a non-technical audience.
